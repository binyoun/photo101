Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from Photography 101 students. Check here before emailing. Updated every two weeks.

Camera & Gear
Can I use multiple cameras for one assignment?
Yes, as long as each camera supports Manual mode and RAW file saving.
Nov 2026
Is my camera suitable for this course?
Check the Camera Checker. It tells you whether your model has the manual controls and RAW support the course needs.

My camera's date/EXIF metadata is wrong. Will this affect my grade?
Submit your photos as they are. Mention in your submission that the camera date was incorrect, and include behind-the-scenes photos taken on your phone (with the correct date) as backup proof.

Submission
I forgot to take Behind-the-Scenes (BTS) photos at one location.
If you have photos from the location, put them in a folder and screenshot the thumbnails as proof. For future locations, do not forget the BTS photos.
Nov 2026
I missed submitting a required file (e.g. ZIP, Statement of Authorship).
Do not re-submit and create a late submission. Instead, add the missing file as a comment on your existing Canvas submission.
Nov 2026
Extensions & Deadlines
How do I request an extension?
Complete the short-term extension application form and email it to your lecturer no later than one working day before the due date. Extensions are up to 7 calendar days.
Nov 2026
What happens if I submit late without an extension?
A late penalty applies per the course guide. Submit on Canvas as soon as possible and email your lecturer with any supporting proof. The penalty may be reduced based on your circumstances, but for fairness it cannot be fully waived outside the formal Special Consideration process.
Nov 2026
Canvas & Course
I cannot access the Canvas modules.
All modules are open from Week 1. If one will not open for you, email your lecturer with a screenshot.
Nov 2026
Should I message my lecturer through Canvas Inbox?
No. Please use email (bin.youn@rmit.edu.vn) for all communication. Do not use the Canvas message function.
