How to write your email

  • Subject: course code, assignment, and your question in a few words.
  • Say who you are: full name, s-number and campus (SGS or HN).
  • Be specific: what you need, which assignment, and what you have already tried.
  • Attach screenshots, forms or proof if they help.
  • One topic per email, and reply in the same thread if you follow up.

What you learn

Modules

The course runs in six modules on Canvas. Each has lecture videos, a quiz you can retry, and a discussion to share your photos.

M0 Week 1

Getting set up

Camera advice and settings, Lightroom setup, shooting RAW.

M1 Weeks 1 to 2

The fundamentals and the exposure triangle

What is photography? Aperture, shutter speed and ISO, a shooting exercise and the Module 1 quiz.

M2 Week 3

Composition, colour and light

Framing and composition, colour, natural light and time of day.

M3 Week 3

Post-production

Lightroom: import, rate, global and local adjustments, black and white, export.

M4 Weeks 4 to 7

Genres and visual analysis

Still life, landscape, portrait, street and conceptual photography; visual consistency and how to analyse an image.

M5 Weeks 8 to 12

Your final folio

Plan two themes and a moodboard, book your consultation, shoot, select and sequence, keep the edit consistent, export the PDF.

Good to know

Rules and tips

  • No generative AI imagesYou may not use generative AI to create images, or to add or change elements of your images. AI can support research and ideas; reference any AI you use.
  • Get permission to shootYou need permission to photograph on campus. Carry the filming and photography form when shooting in public.
  • Shoot RAW, edit in LightroomModule 0 shows you how to set up your camera and Lightroom before you start.
  • Do every quizThey give instant feedback and you can retry them as often as you like.
  • Share and commentPost in the discussions and comment on classmates' work. Feedback is how you improve.
  • Canvas winsAll times are Vietnam time (ICT). If anything here differs from Canvas, Canvas is correct.
