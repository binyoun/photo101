An asynchronous course

There are no scheduled classes. You work through the modules on Canvas at your own pace, whenever suits you each week. The assignment deadlines are fixed, so keep to the weekly plan below.

You still learn with others: share your photos in the discussions, reply to classmates, and book a consultation when you need help.

Attendance = watching the lecture videos

Your attendance is the share of lecture videos you watch in Modules 1 and 2 (Weeks 1 to 4) and Module 4 (Weeks 5 to 8).

0%50% minimum

Watch less than half and you are marked as not attended.

How to make sure it counts

  1. Find the boxes on each module page that say "Watch the video lecture", with the running time.
  2. Play the video inside Canvas while you are logged in. Viewing is recorded automatically.
  3. Watch to the end. Parts you skip are not counted.
  4. Unsure about your record? Ask your lecturer to check it for you.

How to do well

Do each module's quizThe quizzes give you instant feedback, and you can retry them as often as you like.
Use consultationsThere are no live classes, so book a consultation with your lecturer or an Adobe coach when you get stuck.
Share your photosPost in the discussions and comment on your classmates' work. Feedback is how you improve.
Shoot RAW and edit in LightroomModule 0 shows you how to set up your camera and Lightroom before you start.
Get permission on campusYou need permission to photograph on campus. Carry the filming and photography form when shooting in public.
Use AI only where allowedAI can support research and ideas, never your final work. Reference any AI you use.

All due times are Vietnam time (ICT). Canvas is the official record: if anything here differs from Canvas, Canvas wins. Feedback on each assignment comes back within two weeks of its due date, in Marks on Canvas.
